## Reflection

1. Lessons learned

Working as a QA/Tester in this project taught me that software testing is more than checking whether features work. It requires understanding requirements, creating test cases, checking expected and actual results, identifying bugs, and ensuring the application provides a good user experience. Using AI helped me generate test scenarios, organize test cases, and understand testing approaches more quickly. One thing that improved was my productivity and ability to think of more testing scenarios. However, one thing that became worse was the risk of relying too much on AI suggestions, which may not always match the actual requirements. I learned that AI-generated test cases still need human review and real testing. Communication with developers is also important because clear bug reports help the team fix problems more effectively.

2. Where do I stand in the software industry today, in the AI era?

I believe I am still at the beginning of my journey in the software industry. AI is changing how software is developed and tested, and I see it as a tool that can help me work more efficiently. However, I believe technical knowledge, critical thinking, problem-solving, and understanding software quality are still essential. As a future QA Engineer, I need to learn how to use AI effectively without depending on it completely.

3. My plan for next year and the next five years

Next year, I plan to improve my software testing skills, learn automation testing, practice writing test cases, and gain more experience through projects and internships. In five years, I hope to become a professional QA Engineer with strong testing and automation skills who can contribute to building reliable, secure, and high-quality software.
